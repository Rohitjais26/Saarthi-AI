# Messaging Safety Policy

- Use explicit opt-in by channel.
- Include opt-out text in every outbound message.
- Do not infer or mention sensitive attributes.
- Avoid coercive or manipulative language.
- Enforce quiet hours and frequency caps.
- Require admin approval for campaign launch.
- Route low-connectivity segments to community posters with assisted enrollment.
- Record all outbound/reply/onboarding events for optimization and auditability.
